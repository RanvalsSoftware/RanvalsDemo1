# Changelog

## 19.0.3.0.0 — 2026-09-27

- Renamed the marketplace display title to **Employee QR Attendance**.
- Added weekly, monthly and custom auditable attendance periods.
- Added fixed, split and cross-midnight schedule grouping.
- Added planned, worked, scheduled, outside-schedule and shortfall metrics.
- Added raw/policy-adjusted lateness and early-leave indicators.
- Added missing-day, missing-check-out, pending-overtime and anomaly blockers.
- Added reviewed/locked period workflow and revision snapshots.
- Added live pivot/graph analysis, bounded PDF summary, detailed CSV and payroll-preparation CSV.
- Added formula-injection protection and UTF-8 BOM CSV output.
- Added attendance company snapshots for historical reporting.
- Added period-level flexible-schedule reconciliation and source-change checks before locking.
- Added immutable server-side audit transitions and spreadsheet formula-injection protection.
- Added Odoo Apps English description and application icon.

## 19.0.2.0.0 — 2026-09-27

- Rebuilt the original clinic-bound QR attendance feature as a standalone Odoo
  19 add-on with signed actions, rate limits, device revocation, company rules,
  concurrency protection and automated tests.
